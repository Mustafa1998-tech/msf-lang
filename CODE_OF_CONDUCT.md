# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

MSF Lang and the Mustafa Software Factory community are committed to a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free environment for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, or beliefs.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests, discussions, and reviews.
- Assume good faith; ask clarifying questions instead of making accusations.
- Focus feedback on the technical merits, design trade-offs, and evidence.
- Credit others’ work and give credit where it is due.
- Follow the project’s contribution guidelines and licensing terms.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, or personal attacks
- Discriminatory language, slurs, or hate speech
- Deliberately provocative or inflammatory comments
- Publishing others’ private information (doxxing)
- Spam or off-topic promotion
- Knowingly submitting malicious or plagiarized content

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces: GitHub repositories (issues, PRs, discussions, releases), documentation, community channels associated with the project, and public representation of the project.

## Enforcement

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported to the maintainers by opening a GitHub issue marked `conduct` or, when sensitive, through the contact route described in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) marked as a conduct report.

Maintainers may take any action they deem appropriate, including but not limited to:
- requesting changes to comments or submissions
- issuing a warning
- temporarily or permanently banning a participant from the project spaces
- reporting to hosting providers when required

## Appeals

If you believe a moderation decision was made in error, contact the maintainers with the relevant context. Appeals will be reviewed in good faith.

## Attribution

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant and common open-source community standards, tailored to this project.
