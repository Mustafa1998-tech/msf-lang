# MSF Lang — Brand Concept Review

**Status:** Gate B · awaiting selection  
**Version:** 0.1.0-draft.1  
**Date:** 16 November 2026  
**Scope:** three original logo directions for review. No final brand assets have been produced.

---

## 1. Why this review exists

The MSF Lang identity has to work in places a campaign poster never reaches: a 16 px file icon, a VS Code tab, a terminal window, a Windows taskbar, a package listing, and a documentation header. The mark also has to carry a specific idea — a language that is built and executed, not a generic code bracket.

This document records the three directions, what each one is good at, where each one breaks, and the recommendation for the core mark. The decision at Gate B selects the direction; it does not approve final artwork. Final vectors, optical corrections, wordmark, file icons, exports, and Windows integration follow only after that selection.

### Fixed constraints

- Official palette: `#8B35F5` purple, `#171334` navy, `#00D4FF` cyan, `#FFFFFF` white.
- Original geometric mark built from the letters **M**, **S**, and **F**, or from the idea of execution.
- No generic `</>` bracket, no borrowed mark, no raster trace presented as a vector.
- Must survive light and dark surfaces, one-colour reproduction, and sizes from 16 px to 1024 px.
- The plate, if used, is part of the mark — not a rounded rectangle added behind a letter.

### Evaluation criteria

1. **16 px recognition** — can the mark be identified in a file list or tab?
2. **Silhouette** — does it hold without colour or internal detail?
3. **Surface independence** — does it work on light and dark fields?
4. **One-colour reproduction** — can it be printed, etched, or stamped?
5. **Icon behaviour** — does it work as an app icon, file badge, and favicon?
6. **Story** — does it express “software factory” and “language” without a caption?
7. **Expression** — can it carry a website, launch film, or campaign?

## 2. Concept 01 · Aurora

**File:** [`concepts/concept-01-aurora.svg`](concepts/concept-01-aurora.svg)  
**Idea:** one continuous line of code arriving at a result.

### Construction

A single open stroke draws the M in one gesture: left stem, first valley, centre peak, second valley, right stem. The right stem does not stop at the baseline; it flows into an S-shaped landing and ends on a cyan execution node. The stroke is 26 units on a 256-unit canvas, with round caps and joins. A purple gradient runs from `#A855F7` to `#7C3AED`.

There are no enclosed counters and no separate letter parts. The mark is one path plus one node.

### Strengths

- The most expressive of the three. It has a natural motion story for the website, launch film, and conference identity.
- The open silhouette is distinctive and unlike a standard app badge.
- Strong at large sizes, where the S-landing and the node are both legible.
- Works as a single-colour silhouette; the path itself is the mark.

### Risks

- The S-curl compresses at 16–24 px. At those sizes it reads as an M with a small foot, not as an S.
- The cyan node disappears on a white surface unless it receives a dark ring or a darker cyan.
- The eye can read the terminal as a hook or a flourish rather than as the letter S.
- A final system needs a dedicated small-size cut, or the curl must be removed below a defined size.

### Best use

Expression layer: website hero, launch film, editorial, conference identity, large-format print. Not the primary app icon without a simplified variant.

**Verdict:** strongest personality; weakest at icon sizes.

## 3. Concept 02 · Foundry

**File:** [`concepts/concept-02-foundry.svg`](concepts/concept-02-foundry.svg)  
**Idea:** a module with a spark — the factory and the execution trace in one object.

### Construction

A 224-unit rounded plate (52-unit radius) in deep navy carries a solid white M. The M has flat terminals and a 28-unit stem. Inside the right leg, a cyan S-trace (6.5 units) runs from the upper stem to a 7-unit execution node near the baseline. A 1.5-unit purple rule sits just inside the plate edge.

The plate is the mark. It is not a container added behind a standalone letter.

### Strengths

- The strongest at 16 px. The white M on navy resolves immediately, even when the trace disappears.
- Already behaves like an app icon, a file badge, a favicon, and a terminal glyph. It does not need a second design for those surfaces.
- Self-contained on light and dark fields; the plate carries its own background.
- The “factory module” story is built into the frame, and the cyan trace adds the execution idea without changing the silhouette.
- The most system-ready direction: one mark can cover product, file type, and documentation.

### Risks

- A badge can read as a product icon before it reads as a language. The wordmark and the standalone M must carry the language story.
- Rounded-square plates are common in app design. The M, the trace, and the proportions have to do the differentiating work.
- One-colour reproduction needs a dedicated mono cut; a straight grayscale conversion loses the trace.
- The cyan trace is an accent, not a functional detail at 16 px. That is acceptable, but it should not be described as legible at that size.

### Best use

Core mark: app icon, `.msf` file icon, favicon, taskbar, VS Code icon theme, CLI badge, documentation signature.

**Verdict:** most durable and most useful; recommended core mark.

## 4. Concept 03 · Facet

**File:** [`concepts/concept-03-facet.svg`](concepts/concept-03-facet.svg)  
**Idea:** one letter, four planes — the language as a designed object.

### Construction

The M silhouette is cut from a clipped polygon and divided into four purple planes (`#4C1D95`, `#6D28D9`, `#8B35F5`, `#C4B5FD`). A cyan bevel runs along the top-right shoulder, and a cyan node sits in the lower valley. A faint lavender outline defines the silhouette on dark surfaces.

### Strengths

- The most premium and the most distinctive at large sizes. It has a real material quality.
- The silhouette holds at 16 px even when the facet tones collapse into one purple.
- The strongest launch-poster and merchandise presence.
- The cyan bevel and node give the mark a precise, engineered feel without a literal factory metaphor.

### Risks

- The facet tones compress at small sizes; the mark becomes a flat purple M, which is still usable but loses its signature.
- On a dark surface the silhouette needs the outline, or the dark planes disappear into the background.
- A light-surface variant needs a separate palette or a stronger outline.
- It is less immediate than Foundry at 16 px, and less expressive than Aurora in motion.

### Best use

Campaign layer: launch posters, merchandise, editorial covers, special editions, conference materials.

**Verdict:** best campaign mark; not the core system mark.

## 5. Comparison

| Criterion | 01 · Aurora | 02 · Foundry | 03 · Facet |
|---|---|---|---|
| 16 px recognition | At risk — curl collapses | **Strong** — M resolves immediately | Good — silhouette holds, facets drop |
| 24–32 px | Good with simplified cut | **Strong** | Good |
| Light and dark | Good; cyan node needs work | **Strong** — plate is self-contained | Good; needs light-surface variant |
| One colour | **Strong** — path is the mark | Medium — needs a mono cut | **Strong** — silhouette survives |
| App / file icon | Good, loses signature | **Strong** — already icon-shaped | Good, needs simplification |
| Website / editorial | **Strong** — best hero mark | Good — repeatable system mark | **Strong** — best campaign mark |
| Motion / launch film | **Strong** — one continuous line | Medium — static module | Good — light can move across facets |
| “Software factory” story | Medium — flow and execution | **Strong** — the module is the factory | Medium — construction, not production |
| Distinctiveness | **Strong** | Medium — badge category is crowded | **Strong** |

This is a qualitative review, not a numeric score. “At risk” means the concept needs a dedicated variant before it can be used at that size.

## 6. Recommendation

**Adopt Concept 02 · Foundry as the core mark for MSF Lang v0.1.**

It is the only direction that is unmistakable at 16 px without a special redesign, it already works as an app and file badge, and its M plus execution trace survives the loss of colour and detail. Those are the constraints that decide a language identity, because most encounters with `.msf` will happen in a file list, an editor tab, or a terminal — not on a launch poster.

Keep the other two as deliberate layers, not as alternate core marks:

- **Aurora** remains the expression layer: website hero, launch film, editorial, conference identity.
- **Facet** remains the campaign layer: posters, merchandise, special editions.

Do not blend the three into one mark. A blended mark would lose Aurora's motion, Foundry's small-size clarity, and Facet's material quality at the same time. A family with one core mark and two clearly bounded expression layers is stronger than a compromise.

### What follows Gate B

After the core mark is selected:

1. Final vector construction on a defined grid, with optical corrections.
2. Wordmark and horizontal/vertical lockups.
3. Detailed and simplified file icons for `.msf`.
4. Light, dark, and one-colour variants.
5. PNG exports at 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 128, 256, 512, and 1024 px.
6. Multi-size ICO for Windows.
7. VS Code language configuration and icon theme, with activation verified.
8. `BRAND_GUIDELINES.md` with clear space, minimum sizes, colour rules, and misuse examples.
9. Installer and file-association design — no registry changes without explicit Gate D approval.

## 8. ملخص عربي

### الغاية

هذه المراجعة تختار **الاتجاه الأساسي** لهوية MSF Lang قبل إنتاج أي أصول نهائية. العلامة يجب أن تعمل في أيقونة ملف بحجم 16 بكسل، وفي تبويب VS Code، وفي الطرفية، وعلى موقع الإطلاق. الألوان ثابتة: `#8B35F5` و`#171334` و`#00D4FF` و`#FFFFFF`.

### المفاهيم الثلاثة

- **01 · الشفق:** خط واحد متصل يرسم حرف M ثم ينحني إلى هبوط على شكل S وينتهي بنقطة تنفيذ سماوية. الأقوى تعبيرًا وحركة، والأضعف في الأحجام الصغيرة.
- **02 · المسبك:** حرف M أبيض صلب داخل وحدة كحلية داكنة، مع أثر سماوي على شكل S داخل الساق اليمنى ونقطة تنفيذ. الأوضح في 16 بكسل، والأصلح كأيقونة تطبيق وملف.
- **03 · الوجه:** حرف M مقسّم إلى أربعة مستويات هندسية، مع شطبة سماوية ونقطة في الوادي السفلي. الأقوى في الملصقات والحملات، ويحتاج نسخة خاصة للخلفيات الفاتحة.

### التوصية

اعتماد **02 · المسبك** علامة أساسية للإصدار 0.1، مع الإبقاء على **01 · الشفق** طبقة تعبير للموقع والإطلاق، و**03 · الوجه** طبقة حملة للملصقات والمنتجات. لا تُدمج الاتجاهات الثلاثة في علامة واحدة.

### قرار البوابة B

أجب في المحادثة بأحد الخيارات: `Concept 01` أو `Concept 02` أو `Concept 03` أو `Revise …` مع وصف التغيير المطلوب. اختيار المفهوم يعتمد الاتجاه فقط؛ الأصول النهائية وتكامل Windows والمحرك تبقى بوابات موافقة منفصلة.
